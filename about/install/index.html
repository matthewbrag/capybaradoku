How to install Capybaradoku

Capybaradoku is a web app — no app store, no download, no account. Add it to your home screen and it launches full-screen and plays offline.

Open Capybaradoku →

📱 iPhone & iPad (Safari)

  1. Open capybaradoku.com in Safari (this only works in Safari on iOS).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an up-arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The capybara icon appears on your home screen.

Launch it from that icon and it opens full-screen, with no Safari address bar.

🤖 Android (Chrome)

  1. Open capybaradoku.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right).
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Confirm. It installs like a normal app and works offline.

You may also see an automatic “Install” prompt at the bottom of the screen — just tap it.

💻 Desktop (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open capybaradoku.com in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar (a small monitor/⊕ icon on the right).
  3. Click Install. It opens in its own window and gets a desktop/taskbar shortcut.

Why install it?

  • 📴 Works offlineSolve puzzles with no connection once installed.
  • ⚡ Full-screenLaunches like a real app — no browser chrome.
  • 🚫 Still no adsInstalled or not, it's always free and ad-free.
